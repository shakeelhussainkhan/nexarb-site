Getting Started

What is NexArb?
NexArb is an AI-powered arbitrage platform that automatically scans 14,000+ retail products daily, verifies margins across Amazon FBA and Walmart WFS, and routes each deal to the most profitable channel. When both channels are gated, it finds equivalent products on Alibaba for private label sourcing.
Do I need an Amazon or Walmart account to use NexArb?
Yes. NexArb is designed for active Amazon FBA and/or Walmart WFS sellers. You'll need at least one active seller account to connect. You can start with just Amazon and add Walmart later.
How long does setup take?
Most sellers are fully set up within 15-30 minutes. The onboarding wizard guides you through connecting your Amazon SP-API credentials, Walmart API keys, and setting your deal filters. After that, NexArb runs automatically.
Is there a free trial?
Yes! All new accounts receive a 14-day free trial of the Professional plan ($199/mo value). No credit card is required to start. You'll only be charged if you add a payment method and don't cancel before the trial ends.

How It Works

How does NexArb find deals?
NexArb integrates with Tactical Arbitrage to scan retail store products and compares them against Amazon and Walmart marketplace prices. It checks gating status via Amazon SP-API and Walmart Seller API, calculates real margins including all fees, and presents only profitable opportunities.
How accurate are the margins?
NexArb uses real-time marketplace prices and applies actual Amazon FBA and Walmart WFS fee schedules. We also apply a 50% Walmart floor filter — any deal must be profitable even if the Walmart price is 50% lower than the Amazon price. Deals are labeled "Verified" (real-time prices confirmed) or "Estimated" (based on scan-time data).
What is the Alibaba sourcing feature?
When a product is gated on both Amazon and Walmart, NexArb automatically searches Alibaba for equivalent products suitable for private label sourcing. It calculates potential margins, minimum order quantities, and sends you a sourcing opportunity alert with a direct Alibaba search link.
How often does NexArb scan?
NexArb runs three automated scans daily — at 5:30 AM, 1:30 PM, and 9:30 PM Pacific Time. The AI reviewer runs 15 minutes after each scan to score and rank the top opportunities. You can also trigger a manual scan from the dashboard at any time.

About NexArb

Built by sellers, for sellers.

NexArb was founded in 2026 by Shakeel Hussain Khan, a San Jose-based entrepreneur who spent months manually hunting for arbitrage deals across Amazon and Walmart. Frustrated by the time required and the lack of tools that worked across multiple channels simultaneously, he built ArbitrAI — a fully automated deal-sourcing engine.

NexArb is the commercial platform built on top of that engine — bringing the same automation, AI scoring, and multi-channel routing to serious sellers everywhere.

Our Mission

To democratize arbitrage by giving every seller access to enterprise-grade AI and automation — so you can focus on growing your business, not hunting for deals.

What Makes Us Different

  • Triple-channel routing — the only platform that routes across Amazon, Walmart, and Alibaba automatically
  • Verified margins — real prices, not estimates, with a 50% Walmart floor filter
  • Claude AI — powered by Anthropic's Claude Haiku for intelligent deal scoring
  • Zero manual work — fully automated from scan to alert, 3x daily

Our Stack

NexArb is built on Next.js, Supabase, Clerk, Stripe, and Anthropic's Claude API. Our deal engine runs on DigitalOcean with PM2 process management and real-time WebSocket updates.
